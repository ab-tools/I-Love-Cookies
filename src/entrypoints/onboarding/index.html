Welcome to I Love Cookies

What this extension does

On every website you visit, I Love Cookies finds the cookie consent banner and answers it with the maximum consent offered ("Accept all") – on your behalf, automatically.

This means you agree, for every site, to:

  • all cookies and similar technologies (local storage, device identifiers),
  • analytics, profiling and personalised advertising,
  • data sharing with all listed partners/vendors – often hundreds per site.

In return, websites work completely: videos, maps, embedded content, comments and logins are no longer blocked, and you never see a cookie banner again.

You can pause the extension per site, and withdraw your consent for a site at any time from the toolbar popup ("Withdraw consent" deletes that site's cookies and shows the banner again).

"Consent or pay" walls

Some sites let you choose between accepting tracking and a paid subscription. I Love Cookies always chooses the free option ("accept and continue with ads"). It never selects a paid option, never subscribes and never logs in for you.
